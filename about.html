WHO WE ARE

Your Journey Across Kenya Starts With Us

Little Monks Safaris is a Kenyan travel and tourism company focused on creating memorable journeys across Kenya. From wildlife safaris and day trips to transfers, corporate transport and personalized tours, we help travellers experience Kenya with comfort, convenience and a sense of adventure.

We believe that travelling is about more than simply reaching a destination. It is about the places you discover, the people you meet, the landscapes you experience and the memories you take home.

OUR STORY

Built Around
The Spirit Of Kenya

Kenya is a country of extraordinary contrasts. From the open plains of the Maasai Mara and the shadow of Mount Kilimanjaro to the Great Rift Valley and the Indian Ocean coast, every journey reveals something different.

Little Monks Safaris was created around the idea of making these experiences easier to discover while giving every traveller a journey that feels personal.

Whether you are visiting Kenya for the first time, returning for another safari or simply looking for a different way to experience the country, our goal is to make the journey itself part of the memory.
